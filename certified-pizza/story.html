[ The story so far ]
FAMILY · FIRST OVEN · ST MARYS

It started with one oven, one recipe, and the stubborn belief that a pizza ought to be honest.

Two brothers, a Nonna's recipe book, and a custom stone-deck oven that took three days to install. We opened in St Marys because it's home — and because the Nepean deserved a pizza place that took the craft as seriously as any Sydney CBD venue.

From day one we've done things the long way around. Doughs cold-fermented for 48 hours. Tomatoes from San Marzano because nothing else tastes like them. Mozzarella torn by hand because a knife crushes the curd. We don't cut corners — even on the 30-inch.

Today we're still family-run. You'll usually find one of us in the kitchen, one of us out front, and Nonna telling everyone what they're doing wrong. Come hungry — and stay a while.

Four chapters · still being written
'18

The Idea

Two brothers, three margheritas a week at the kitchen table, and Nonna's recipe book open to page 14. The plan started here.

'20

The Oven

A custom stone-deck oven was installed in St Marys on a Tuesday. It took three days to commission. We've never moved it since.

'21

First Slice

We opened our doors at 5 Crana Street. Margherita, marinara, and a quiet Tuesday night. By Saturday there was a queue out the door.

'24

The 30-inch

We built a custom peel and rebuilt our oven floor to fit it. The first 30-inch came out for a 21st birthday. It's been on the menu ever since.

[ Why street names? ]

A pizza for

Each pie on our menu carries the name of a road, a heritage corner, or a thoroughfare that built the Nepean. A small tip of the cap to home.

№ 01

Queen Street → Margherita

The main vein of St Marys. Honest, busy, classic. Our margherita carries the name.

№ 02

Great Western → Meat Lover

The highway that connects everything. A heavyweight pie for a heavyweight road.

№ 03

Mamre Road → The Monster

Long, loud and unmissable. Lent its name to our 30-inch signature.

№ 04

Charles Hackett → All-meat

A working-man's road. We named the all-meat 30-inch combo after it.

№ 05

Phillip Street → Quattro Formaggi

Quiet, refined, full of character. Four cheeses, no apologies.

№ 06

Henry Lawson → Prosciutto

Named for the poet. San Daniele prosciutto, rocket, lemon, parm.

[ House rules ]

Three things
we don't
compromise on.

01

Time on the dough.

48 hours of cold ferment. No shortcuts, no chemical accelerants. The flavour comes from the wait.

02

Heat in the oven.

The deck runs hot — over 350°C. Every pizza cooks fast, blistered at the cornicione, soft in the centre. The way it should.

03

Hands on the table.

A pizzeria is a place to sit down. We make sure every guest is greeted, fed, and remembered next time they walk in.
